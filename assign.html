Assignments

Practice, quizzes and tests from the standards bank. Students type a code, tap their name and work on their Chromebooks. Every attempt is saved, and results go to Student Progress.

How grading works
  • Grade and complete are two numbers. Grade is points earned. Complete is how much a student attempted. Both show everywhere.
  • Scores only go up. In practice a miss costs nothing: the student gets a hint, another try, then a fresh problem. Misses, tries, viewed solutions and time are kept as effort, never taken out of a grade.
  • Practice is mastery. Each skill asks for a number right (3 unless you change it). Problems come from that standard's bank in each student's own order, so neighbours see different problems.
  • "Still stuck?" shows the worked steps and the answer. That problem then doesn't count, and a new one comes up, so looking is never a shortcut.
  • Quizzes and tests go to students without the answer key. This page checks them. Students see nothing until you release scores (and, if you choose, the answers).
  • Test corrections give each student only the questions they missed. Half of the points they win back (you can change it) raises the test: 70% → 85%.
  • Names. Students tap their name from your Rosters page (first name and last initial). There are no student accounts.
  • Nothing here is normed. No grade levels or percentiles: just what your students did on your problems.
