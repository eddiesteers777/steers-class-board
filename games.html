Getting your sets…

New question set

One line for each question: CATEGORY | POINTS | QUESTION | ANSWER. Up to 6 categories with 6 questions each. Add a line with the category FINAL for a final wager question. Type exponents as 10^4 and fractions as 3/4; the board shows them properly.

Import from Blooket, Wayground, Gimkit, Kahoot or a spreadsheet

Choose the CSV file you downloaded, or open the sheet, select the cells (headings included) and paste them below. For an Excel file, open it in Excel or Google Sheets and copy the cells. The questions land in the table below for you to check before saving.
