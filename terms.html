Who can use it

Anchor Board is for teachers and school staff. Students use it only through activities their teacher runs, such as a student game or live notes, and never make their own accounts.

Your account

You sign in with Google. Keep your sign-in private. Don't share your account with students.

Your content

What you add (lessons, question sets, drawings, rosters, results) stays yours. You give us permission to store and show it only so Anchor Board can work for you. You can delete it at any time from Settings, Your data.

Only add content you have the right to use. Class Pages are public, so never put student information, answer keys or tests on them.

Student information

Follow your school's rules about student data. Add only what you need, and use first names and last initials where you can. If your school needs a signed student data privacy agreement, ask us before you add student information.
