Weekly backup and first-time setup

Home shows a reminder when your last backup is a week old. One tap backs up. (A fully automatic backup with nothing to tap would need a paid server, so it is not built.)

Which Google account? If you pick your school Google account, the files sit in the district's Google Drive, where student work is already allowed. Your personal account works too. A school account can be blocked by the district's Google settings; if so, Anchor Board says so.

If Anchor Board says Google Drive is not turned on, it is a free one-time switch:

  1. Open Google Drive API for the steers-class-board project, signed in with the Google account that owns Anchor Board.
  2. Click Enable. No billing is needed.
  3. Wait a minute, then tap Back up again.
