Anchor Board Back to the Remote

Show the board on another screen

Any TV, Chromebook or computer with a web browser can show your board. It follows your Remote live and can't change anything.

Sign in on this device first

Linking a screen needs your teacher account. Sign in, then scan the code on the TV again.

Sign in

1Link a screen with its code

On the TV or computer, open anchorboard.web.app/tv. It shows a code and a QR. Scan the QR with this phone, or type the code here.

2Or cast to a Chromecast or Google TV

From Chrome on a Chromebook or computer, on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. Pick the TV, and it links on its own.

Linked screens

    AirPlay, Miracast and screen mirroring

    A website can't start AirPlay (Apple TV) or Miracast (many smart TVs and Windows) by itself. For those, open the board on this device and use the device's own mirroring:

    • iPhone or iPad: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, pick the Apple TV.
    • Windows laptop: press Windows key + K and pick the TV.
    • Chromebook or Chrome: the Chrome menu, then Cast, then Cast tab.

    The code above is usually better: the TV runs the board itself, so your phone stays free.
