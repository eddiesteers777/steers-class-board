Settings

Site colors

Pick the colors for your teacher pages on this device and your others. The projected board keeps its own Board look.

Bell times

Enter when each period starts and ends. The top panel then follows the clock and shows the class you are teaching. Keep more than one schedule (activity days, early dismissal) and pick which one runs today. Tomorrow goes back to Regular on its own.

Other times in this schedule

Lunch blocks, gym dismissal, dismissal, anything with a time. They show under Today on this page.

Plan ahead

Activity day or early dismissal coming up? Pick the date and its schedule. That day starts on it by itself, and the board's period clock and bell timeline use it.
