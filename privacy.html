Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Anchor Board is a classroom board, remote and planner for teachers. It is made by Edgar Steers, a middle school math teacher in Alabama. This page says what Anchor Board collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version. Teachers sign in. Students never make accounts. We collect as little about students as we can: a first name and last initial during a game, and whatever a teacher chooses to save. We never sell data, never show ads, and never use student data to train AI. A teacher can delete everything with one button.

Who this covers

Teachers who sign in with Google. Students who join a game or live notes with a code from their teacher. Visitors to the public site, including anyone who joins the early access list.

What we collect from teachers

Your Google account. Your name, email address and profile photo, so you can sign in.

What you add. Your classes, periods and bell times, lessons and weeks, question sets, whiteboard drawings, rosters, class results, scanned student papers, and settings. You choose what to add.

What we collect about students

Students never create an account, never give an email address, and never sign in with Google.

Student games and live notes. A student joins on their own device with a code. Their device gets an anonymous guest sign-in with no name, email or password attached. While the game or live notes session is open, we keep the name the student picks (first name and last initial), their answers, their score, and anything they draw on the work pad. When the teacher closes the game or ends live notes, all of it is deleted.

Anything a teacher saves. A teacher may choose to save student names, scores and scanned papers in their own private account, for example in rosters or class results. Only that teacher can see it. We ask teachers to use first names and last initials, and to save only what they need.

Class Pages. A teacher can post a student copy of a week (agenda, learning targets, practice problems, links) so students and families can see it without signing in. Class Pages never contain student names, answers or grades.

How we use it

Only to run Anchor Board for the teacher who signed in: to show their board, run their remote, run their games, and keep their own records. We do not build profiles of students, we do not track students across other sites, and there are no ads.

We do not use any teacher or student data to train artificial intelligence models.

Schools and student records (FERPA)

When a teacher uses Anchor Board for their school, we act for the school. Student information stays under the school's control, is used only for the school's purposes, and is never shared with anyone else except as described on this page or as the law requires. A school or district can ask us to sign its student data privacy agreement.

Children under 13 (COPPA)

Anchor Board is for teachers, not for children to use on their own. When a teacher uses a student game or live notes in class, the school gives consent on the parents' behalf, for classroom use only. We collect only what the activity needs, and we delete game data when the teacher closes the game. A parent can ask their child's teacher or school to review or delete their child's information, or contact us below.

Who can see it

Each teacher can see only their own data. During a game, students see the class name list (first names and last initials) and can change only their own entry. Anyone with the link can see a teacher's Class Pages, which never hold student information. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Service providers. Anchor Board runs on Google Firebase (sign-in, database and hosting). Google stores the data for us and may not use it for its own purposes. Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your device's IP address.

How we protect it

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is encrypted where Google stores it. Database rules lock each teacher's data to that teacher's own sign-in, and let a student guest write only their own game entry. Student data is never kept in the site's public code.

If a security breach ever affects personal information, we will tell the affected teachers and schools without unreasonable delay, and as required by law.

Keeping and deleting data

Games and live notes are deleted when the teacher closes them.

Teacher data is kept until the teacher deletes it. In Settings, Your data shows everything your account has saved, and Delete all my data erases all of it, including any open game and your posted Class Pages.

Schools can ask us to delete a teacher's or the school's data. We will do so within 30 days and confirm in writing.

Early access list

If you join the early access list, we keep what you enter: your email, your first name if you give it, what you teach, your classroom screen and device, and any note. We use it only to contact you about Anchor Board. Ask and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how student information is used, we will tell teachers before it takes effect.
