Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Anchor Board is made by Edgar Steers, a middle school math teacher in Alabama. Anchor Board is a classroom board, remote, and planner for teachers.

Information Anchor Board collects

Your teacher account. When you sign in with Google, Anchor Board receives your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account.

What you add. Lesson plans, bell times, rosters, class data, whiteboard drawings, and anything else you add while using Anchor Board.

Student information. Teachers may add student names and scores, for example in rosters and class results. Students do not create accounts.

Student games. When a teacher runs a student game, students join with a game code on their own device. Anchor Board gives that device an anonymous guest sign-in with no name, email or password. While the game is open it keeps the name the student picks (first name and last initial), their answers, their score and the pictures they draw on the work pad. Closing the game deletes all of it. A teacher can choose to keep each student's name and score in their own private class results.

How it is used

Anchor Board uses this information only to run Anchor Board for the teacher who signed in: to show the board, run the remote, and keep that teacher's own records.

Early access list

If you join the early access list on the public site, Anchor Board keeps what you enter there: your email, your first name if you give it, what you teach, your classroom screen and device, and any note you write. It is used only to contact you about early access. Ask and it will be deleted.

Sharing

Anchor Board does not sell personal information. Data is stored with Google Firebase, which provides the sign-in and storage Anchor Board runs on. Nothing is shared with advertisers or other companies. Each teacher can only see their own data.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while you use Anchor Board. You can delete your own class data from the site. To delete everything tied to your account, email Edgar Steers at eddiesteers777@gmail.com.

Children

Anchor Board is made for teachers. Students never create accounts. In a student game they use an anonymous guest sign-in on their own device, and their game data is deleted when the teacher closes the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.
