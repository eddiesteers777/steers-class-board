Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

AnchorBoard is made by Edgar Steers, a middle school math teacher in Alabama. AnchorBoard is a classroom board, remote, and planner for teachers.

Information AnchorBoard collects

Your teacher account. When you sign in with Google, AnchorBoard receives your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account.

What you add. Lesson plans, bell times, rosters, class data, whiteboard drawings, and anything else you add while using AnchorBoard.

Student information. Teachers may add student names and scores, for example in rosters and class results. Students do not create accounts.

Student games. When a teacher runs a student game, students join with a game code on their own device. AnchorBoard gives that device an anonymous guest sign-in with no name, email or password. While the game is open it keeps the name the student picks (first name and last initial), their answers, their score and the pictures they draw on the work pad. Closing the game deletes all of it. A teacher can choose to keep each student's name and score in their own private class results.

How it is used

AnchorBoard uses this information only to run AnchorBoard for the teacher who signed in: to show the board, run the remote, and keep that teacher's own records.

Sharing

AnchorBoard does not sell personal information. Data is stored with Google Firebase, which provides the sign-in and storage AnchorBoard runs on. Nothing is shared with advertisers or other companies. Each teacher can only see their own data.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while you use AnchorBoard. You can delete your own class data from the site. To delete everything tied to your account, email Edgar Steers at eddiesteers777@gmail.com.

Children

AnchorBoard is made for teachers. Students never create accounts. In a student game they use an anonymous guest sign-in on their own device, and their game data is deleted when the teacher closes the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.
