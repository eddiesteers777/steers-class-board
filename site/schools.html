For schools

Plain answers about accounts and data. If something here doesn't answer your question, ask before you start.

Who signs in

Only teachers. A teacher signs in with Google. Students never make an account and never sign in with an email.

Student games

When a teacher runs a student game, students join on their own device with a game code. The device gets an anonymous guest sign-in with no name, email or password. While the game is open it keeps the name the student picks (first name and last initial), their answers, their score and what they draw on the work pad. Closing the game deletes all of it.

What's on the board

Nothing that names a student goes on the board unless the teacher puts it there, for example with the name picker or groups.

Where data lives

  • Each teacher's lessons, rosters, class data and drawings are stored in that teacher's own private space. Other teachers can't see them.
  • Storage and sign-in run on Google Firebase.
  • No ads. No selling data. Nothing is shared with advertisers or other companies.

Sub days

The guest teacher view runs the board on one device with no sign-in. It saves nothing and never touches the teacher's account.

Your district may have its own rules for classroom software and student data. Check with your school before you add student names or scores.
