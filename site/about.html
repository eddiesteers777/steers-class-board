About

Built by a teacher, for a real classroom.

How it started

I teach 7th and 8th grade math in Alabama. Every day I was building slides for three different classes and clicking through them at the front of the room.

So I built a board instead. The agenda, the bellringer, the notes and the timers live in one place, and I run it from my Chromebook while I walk the room.

What I care about

  • Calm. One screen, one job. Nothing covers the lesson.
  • Fast in class. If a tool makes a teacher stop and think mid-lesson, it gets cut.
  • Students first. No student accounts. Nothing about a student goes on the board unless the teacher puts it there.

Where it's going

Right now it runs my classes. Next, it opens to other teachers, a few at a time. If you want in, join the list.
