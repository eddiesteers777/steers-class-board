  1. The bell rings

    Bell timeline

    The board switches to the bellringer on its own when the bell rings, timer and all. You can still change screens by hand.

  2. Notes, one step at a time

    Live notes

    Worked examples show one step and the reason for it. Students answer a check-in on their Chromebooks, and the ring fills as answers come in.

  3. A game that teaches

    Vault Raid

    Students crack each other's vaults by solving problems. Miss one and they get the next step, not the answer. Each step costs a few coins.

  4. Know who needs you tomorrow

    Student Progress

    Every answer lands under its standard. You see who needs help, on what, and what to do next.

  • Google DriveBack up your plans, grades and scanned student work to your own Drive.

  • Google sign-inTeachers sign in with the Google account they already have.
