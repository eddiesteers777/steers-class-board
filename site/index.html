How it works

Plan the week once. After that, class runs from one screen.

  1. Plan the week

    Each day gets an agenda, a learning target, bellringer problems with answers, notes and reminders. Build it once and it's ready for every period.

  2. Put the board up

    Open the board on the projector or interactive panel. It shows the right day for the class in front of you.

  3. Teach from the Remote

    On a Chromebook or phone, tap Next, show an answer, start a timer or launch a game. The board follows right away.

What's on the board

Everything a class period needs, in the order you teach it.

Bellringers with a built-in timer

One problem at a time, big enough to read from the back row. Reveal the answer when you're ready.

Guided notes, one step at a time

Targets, vocabulary, the big idea, then worked examples that show one step at a time with the reason for each step. A whiteboard sits right next to it.

  • Students see the step and the why.
  • Write on the board with a pen, or add a graph, number line, ruler or protractor.

Speed dating, timed for you

The steps stay on screen. Round and check timers run on the board, with sound cues, so you can walk the room.
