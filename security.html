Security and privacy at a glance

For school and district technology staff. Last updated: November 18, 2026

The full details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms. Questions: Edgar Steers, eddiesteers777@gmail.com.

Who signs inTeachers, with Google. Students never make accounts or sign in with Google.
Student data collectedDuring a game or live notes: the name the student picks (first name and last initial), answers, score and work-pad drawings. Deleted when the teacher closes it. Anything else is saved only if the teacher chooses to.
Not collectedStudent email, student ID, birth date, home address, or photos of students. A student guest gets a random sign-in ID that is not linked to their name outside the game.
Where it's storedGoogle Firebase (Firestore database, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Hosting).
EncryptionHTTPS for all traffic. Encrypted at rest by Google.
Who can see itDatabase rules lock each teacher's data to that teacher's sign-in. A student guest can write only their own game entry. Class Pages are public and never hold student information.
Ads, selling, AI trainingNone. No advertising, no selling or renting data, and no use of student data to train AI.
TrackingNo analytics or tracking scripts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts.
DeletionTeachers delete everything from Settings, Your data. Schools can ask for deletion and get written confirmation within 30 days.
Breach noticeAffected teachers and schools are told without unreasonable delay, and as the law requires.
FERPA and COPPAActs for the school as a school official. Classroom use relies on school consent for students under 13. We can review your district's student data privacy agreement.
Works withChromebooks, laptops, iPads and phones in a current browser. Students join with a 6-digit code or a QR code. No app to install.
